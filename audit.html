Why ayitianavan.com looks unfinished, and how the redesign fixes it

The site has good, regular reporting. The problem is presentation: the homepage gives no sense of what matters today, the header spends its space on a weather widget, and several blocks are empty or out of date. Readers judge credibility in seconds, and right now the layout works against the journalism.

How to put this on the real site

  1. Keep WordPress. All your articles, categories and authors stay where they are. Only the theme changes.
  2. Build a custom theme (or a block theme) from this mockup, replacing the Elementor layouts. The homepage pulls the lead story, "À la minute" and section blocks from your existing categories.
  3. Live TV: stream through YouTube Live (free, easiest) or a provider such as Mux or Vimeo Livestream, then embed the player where the mockup shows it.
  4. Shorts: upload vertical videos as a new "Short" post type in WordPress (or pull them from your YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels feed), and the rail and vertical player display them automatically.
  5. Language: add Kreyòl with a translation plugin such as Polylang so the FR / KREYÒL switch works.

Estimated effort: about 2–3 weeks for a developer to turn the mockup into a working WordPress theme, plus setup for the live stream.
